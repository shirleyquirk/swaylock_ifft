tryseven.c is an invertible relative of trysix.c: a 2-D DCT of Y, Cb, Cr instead of |FFT| of H, S, V. No phase is thrown away, so untryseven gets the image back (≈40 dB on a desktop screenshot). It needs the exact pixels: a PNG screenshot at the original size, not a resized or JPEG copy.
